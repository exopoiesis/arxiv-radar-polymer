# Discretely self-similar solutions to coupled fluid systems with locally $L^2$ data

**Authors:** Chen-Chih Lai, Tai-Peng Tsai

In this paper we present a systematic way to construct discretely self-similar solutions of fluid systems, including the viscous resistive magnetohydrodynamics equations, the viscoelastic Navier-Stokes equations with damping, and the Oberbeck-Boussinesq system, for locally $L^2$ initial data which are not necessarily in the uniformly local $L^2$ space. The solutions satisfy local energy inequalities, local a priori bounds depending only on the $L^2$ norm of the initial data in a fixed ball (and, for the Oberbeck--Boussinesq system, on the force), and a limiting formula for the pressure.
