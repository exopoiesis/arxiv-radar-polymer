# From dynamic strain aging to Alessandro--Beatrice--Bertotti--Montorsi dynamics of propagating Portevin--Le Chatelier bands

**Authors:** Tero Mäkinen, Mikko J. Alava

Type-A Portevin--Le Chatelier bands are propagating strain localizations whose velocity exhibits strong intermittent fluctuations. We derive a coarse-grained connection between the dynamic-strain-aging model of Hähner and Rizzi and the Alessandro--Beatrice--Bertotti--Montorsi (ABBM) mean-field model of avalanche dynamics. A propagating band is reduced to a single phase coordinate while retaining the elastic feedback of the tensile machine. This feedback generates the increasing drive and restoring terms of the ABBM description. A finite-time adjoint calculation determines the stress mobility of the band and is checked independently by direct stress continuation. The mobility is approximately proportional to the hardening parameter, whereas the band speed scales approximately as its square root. Short-range heterogeneity in the plastic strain deposited by the band is converted by the global machine feedback into a Brownian effective force. The resulting reduction provides a microscopic route from dynamic strain aging to ABBM-like avalanche dynamics of propagating PLC bands.
