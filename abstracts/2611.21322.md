# RevealLO: Global-Scale FWI Including Long-Period Major-Arc Surface Wave and Multi-Orbit Body Wave Data

**Authors:** C. J. Schiller, Scott Keating, Michael Afanasiev, Patrick Marty, Andreas Fichtner

Global full-waveform inversion (FWI) has matured significantly in recent years, but resolution in the southern hemisphere often remains limited due to sparse receiver coverage. To address this problem, we constructed RevealLO, a global-scale FWI model that updates its predecessor REVEAL by expanding the inversion time window from 1 hour to 3.5 hours. The extended time window incorporates both minor-arc ($R_1, G_1$) and major-arc ($R_2, G_2$) surface wave arrivals, as well as late-arriving multi-orbit body waves, thereby increasing azimuthal coverage beneath ocean basins and the southern hemisphere. We simulate 3-D viscoelastic wavefields using the spectral-element solver Salvus on global fourth-order cubed-sphere meshes, and update velocity structure by successively broadening the period band from 100 - 200 s to 60 - 200 s. Measurements and adjoint sources are based on time-frequency phase misfits, which are suitable for both large-amplitude surface and low-amplitude multiply-reflected body waves. For iterative optimization, we employ a mini-batch stochastic trust-region L-BFGS algorithm, followed by mesh reprojection onto the $33~s$ REVEAL baseline mesh and lowermost-mantle regularization. RevealLO preserves short-period whole-Earth body wave fits while improving azimuthal sampling and mitigating trade-offs between unmodeled azimuthal anisotropy and isotropic structure in the upper and mid mantle.
