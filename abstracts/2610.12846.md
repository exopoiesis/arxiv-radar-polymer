# Global Well-Posedness and Energy Decay for an Abstract Viscoelastic Wave Equation with Nonlocal or Friction Damping

**Authors:** Jun Zhou, Teng Wang

This paper investigates an abstract viscoelastic wave equation in a separable Hilbert space, in which the damping term depends on a power of the velocity norm. Depending on whether the power exponent is zero or nonzero, this damping corresponds to friction damping or nonlocal damping, respectively; the equation also contains a viscoelastic damping represented by an integral term with a relaxation function. We establish global well-posedness for initial data in \(\mathcal{H}_1\) by combining monotone operator theory with the Faedo--Galerkin method, and prove uniqueness for all exponents greater than \(-1\), with the singular range between \(-1\) and \(0\) handled through the monotonicity rather than the Lipschitz continuity of the nonlocal damping operator. We then study the long-time behavior of the energy via integral inequalities, multiplier techniques, and Nakao's method, and obtain explicit decay estimates showing that the energy decays exponentially in the friction case and polynomially in the nonlocal case. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first work to consider the combined effects of viscoelastic damping and nonlocal damping in such an abstract framework, and it is also the first to analyze nonlocal damping with negative exponents.
