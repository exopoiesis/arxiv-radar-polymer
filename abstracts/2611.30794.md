# Pulse-to-pulse Power Fluctuations in Ultra-Fast Fiber Amplifiers with Gain-Managed Nonlinearity

**Authors:** Maksim Kozlov, Colin J. McKinstrie, Nitzan Haviv, Pavel Sidorenko

We present a theoretical framework for predicting pulse-to-pulse power fluctuations caused by pump and seed power noise and by amplified spontaneous emission in ultrafast fiber amplifiers operating in the gain-managed nonlinear regime. The numerical model evaluates statistically averaged quantities directly, without Monte Carlo simulations with stochastic sources, and reduces to a semi-analytical monochromatic model in the limit of vanishing signal bandwidth and negligible ASE. We show that nonlinear spectral broadening of the signal and of its perturbations, combined with the frequency-dependent gain, significantly alters the technical relative intensity noise in both the high-power, high-repetition-rate and the low-power, low-repetition-rate regimes, and that in the latter regime the contributions related to amplified spontaneous emission become comparable to those induced by pump and seed fluctuations.
