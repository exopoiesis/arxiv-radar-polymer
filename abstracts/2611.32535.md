# Odd Viscosity-Driven Phase Separation in Chiral Active Mixtures

**Authors:** Xiaoyi Wu, Tzer Han Tan, Hongbo Zhao

Binary mixtures of active particles spinning in opposite directions can spontaneously separate into homochiral domains, yet the mechanism behind this robust demixing remains poorly understood. Here, we establish a two-fluid hydrodynamic theory that generalizes odd viscosity to multicomponent systems. We show that cross parity-violating viscosities, which couple the stress of one chiral species to the strain rate of another, can destabilize the homogeneous mixture, while odd viscosity within each homochiral domain governs the interfacial stability and dynamics. Microscopic measurements of stress and velocity correlations yield viscosity estimates that predict spontaneous demixing followed by domain coarsening. These findings identify odd viscosity as a route to programming phase behavior and domain morphology in chiral active mixtures.
