# Reliability-based Sizing of Energy Storage for Large Load Integration

**Authors:** Atri Bera, Chandan Chaudhary, Dilip Pandit, Cody J. Newlun, Tu A. Nguyen, Raymond H. Byrne, Joydeep Mitra

The number of large loads connected to the bulk power system has been increasing rapidly worldwide. Emerging high-demand facilities, particularly AI data centers, are introducing new challenges for power system planners. In absence of appropriate planning mechanisms, the addition of high volumes of data center loads will lead to an increase in outages, thus degrading reliability of the system. Energy storage systems (ESS) offer a promising solution to mitigate the reliability impacts of these large loads, due to their flexibility and fast-ramping characteristics. However, ESS should be judiciously sized and sited to maximize the reliability benefits to the grid. This paper presents a framework for the optimal sizing and siting of ESS to minimize the adverse reliability impacts of large load additions. First, a stochastic semi-Markov model is developed to model the load of AI data centers and generate profiles for several such facilities. These profiles are then incorporated into a Markov-chain Monte Carlo simulation-based composite system reliability assessment framework. A detailed nodal network model is used to quantify the reliability impacts of adding data centers and to identify buses most affected by large loads. Resource adequacy metrics are used to evaluate the overall system reliability impacts. ESS are then strategically sized and sited based on this analysis to mitigate the negative impacts of large loads on overall system reliability. The proposed framework is demonstrated using the RTS-GMLC 72-bus test system. The results show how ESS can effectively enhance system reliability in the presence of rapidly growing large-load demand. This framework provides system planners, utilities, and regulators with a practical tool for maintaining reliable power system operation using energy storage as more large loads are integrated into the grid.
