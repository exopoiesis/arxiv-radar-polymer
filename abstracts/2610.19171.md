# A direct theory of strain-gradient special Cosserat rods via the principle of virtual work

**Authors:** R. S. Hallyburton, C. Rodriguez

Motivated by recent advances in the synthesis of effective one-dimensional mechanical metamaterials, we develop a direct theory of strain-gradient special Cosserat rods based on the principle of virtual work. The internal virtual work is formulated intrinsically in terms of variations of the classical Cosserat strains and their spatial derivatives, without requiring a three-dimensional parent material or a stored energy. Objectivity and insensitivity to rigid virtual displacements characterize the admissible internal-work density, while localization yields the dynamic field equations, higher-order contact interactions, natural boundary conditions, and balances of linear and angular momentum. For inextensible and unshearable rods, the constrained principle determines a unique multiplier functional, although its representation by reactive force and double-force fields possesses a gauge freedom absent from classical rod theory. We also formulate objective elastic, hyperelastic, and viscoelastic constitutive laws, extend transverse hemitropy, transverse isotropy, and flip symmetry to strain-gradient rods, and derive invariant representations of symmetric stored energies. As an application, we study a planar inextensible cantilever under a compressive terminal force. We prove that the eigenvalues of the linearized problem are simple and coincide precisely with the loads at which bifurcation from the straight state occurs. In addition, each bifurcation point generates a connected global branch of nonlinear equilibria that is locally analytic near its origin.
