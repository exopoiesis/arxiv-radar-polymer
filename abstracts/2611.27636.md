# Chromatic calorimetry using nanocomposites: status and future challenges

**Authors:** Anne-Mazarine Lyon, Etiennette Auffray, Marco Caporale, Katerina Decka, Michael Doser, Jean-Louis Faure, Greta Gajdan, Yacine Haddad, Jan Kral, Joseph Mariano, Loris Martinazzoli, Emanuele Mazzola, Eva Mihokova, Solangel Rojas Torres, Matteo Salomoni, Vojtech Zabloudil

The development of advanced detector technologies will play a key role in future high-energy physics experiments, such as the FCC-ee. In this context, chromatic calorimetry is an emerging concept for electromagnetic calorimeters, with the potential to combine the precise energy measurement of homogeneous calorimeters with longitudinal reconstruction of particle showers. The state of the art in chromatic calorimetry is reviewed, including experimental results from proof-of-concept prototypes and simulation studies investigating quantum-dot-based configurations. Recent progress and current challenges in the development of nanocomposites are also discussed. Finally, future prospects for chromatic calorimetry are outlined.
