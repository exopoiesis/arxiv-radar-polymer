Fragility and thermal expansion control crystal melting and the glass transition

Alessio Zaccone, Konrad Samwer

arXiv:2503.13270·cond-mat.soft·Published 2025-03-17

Analytical relations for the glass transition temperature, $T_g$, and the crystal melting temperature, $T_m$, are developed on the basis of nonaffine lattice dynamics. The proposed relations explain: (i) the seemingly universal factor of $\approx 2/3$ difference between glass transition temperature and melting temperature of the corresponding crystal, and (ii) the recent empirical discovery that both $T_g$ and $T_m$ are proportional to the liquid fragility $m$ divided by the thermal expansion coefficient $α$ of the solid.
