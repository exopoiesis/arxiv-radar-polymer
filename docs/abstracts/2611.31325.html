Condensates and Edge Effects in the Generalised Takayasu Model

Angad Singh

arXiv:2611.31325·cond-mat.stat-mech·Published 2026-11-23

Recently, in the 1D Takayasu Mass Model (MM) of diffusion-aggregation with input, large condensates and a novel power law were observed in simulations, and later characterised exactly. We extend these studies to the Takayasu model with generalised input, and provide a relation to translate the distribution function of the MM to any model with a space and time independent injection process. In the 1D Charge Model (CM) of a system of size $L$, where $q=+1$ and $-1$ charge monomers are injected with equal probability at each site in each time step, there is a power law distribution of cluster charge $P(q)\sim |q|^{-5/3}$. This distribution cuts off at $|q|\sim L^{3/2}$ in the steady state for long times $t\gg L^2$ and a large charge condensate forms with a net charge between $-\sqrt{Lt}$ and $\sqrt{Lt}$. A distance dependent distribution function is observed around the charge condensate and near open boundaries, both of which act like an edge in the system, analogous to the observations in the MM. The charge distribution measured at a distance $x$ from the edge transitions from the previously known CM bulk power law $P(q)\sim |q|^{-5/3}$ for small clusters ($|q|< x^{3/2}$), to a novel power law $P(q)\sim |q|^{-7/3}$ for larger clusters ($|q|>x^{3/2}$). In the 1D Pair Injection Model (PIM), where $+1$ and $-1$ charge monomers are injected simultaneously at adjacent sites, the power law distribution $P(q)\sim |q|^{-2}$ cuts off at $|q|\sim L$ in the steady state for long times, and no large growing condensate is formed. We still observe a distance dependent distribution measured from the largest clusters, or an open boundary, with a bulk power law $P(q)\sim |q|^{-2}$ for small clusters ($|q|< x$), and an edge power law $P(q)\sim |q|^{-3}$ for larger clusters ($|q >x$).
