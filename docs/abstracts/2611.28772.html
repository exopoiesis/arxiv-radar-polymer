MM-Open5x: Practical Conformal Electronics via Multi-Material Multi-Axis Additive Manufacturing

Iván Revenga Riesco, Freddie Hong, Borut Lampret, Connor Myant, David Boyle

arXiv:2611.28772·cs.ET·Published 2026-11-21

Multi-axis, multi-material additive manufacturing enables the production of conformal electronic systems that are difficult to realise using planar processes and can offer enhanced performance; however, accessibility and reproducibility remain challenging. This paper presents MM-Open5x, an open and low-cost multi-material, multi-axis additive manufacturing workflow for practical conformal electronics. The workflow spans design, simulation, toolpath generation, fabrication, testing, and assembly, and is released alongside complete design files, toolpaths, and representative G-code. Representative case studies demonstrate both the benefits and limitations of multi-axis deposition in practice, explicitly documenting common failure modes, calibration sensitivities, assembly bottlenecks, and material variability. Rather than prescribing an optimal solution, MM-Open5x is presented as a transparent, reproducible foundation upon which a broader community can iterate, extend, and apply conformal electronics workflows across diverse application domains.
