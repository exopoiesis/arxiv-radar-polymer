Coarsening Kinetics in Active Model B+: Macroscale and Microscale Phase Separation

Pradeep Kumar Yadav, Shradha Mishra, Sanjay Puri

arXiv:2506.14548·cond-mat.soft·Published 2025-06-17·Updated 2025-08-27

We perform a comprehensive numerical investigation of the coarsening kinetics of active Brownian particles modeled by the {\it Active Model B+} (AMB+). This model was introduced by Tjhung et al. [Phys. Rev. X {\bf 8}, 031080 (2018)] and is a generalization of Model B for a conserved order parameter, with two additional activity terms. These terms correspond to rotation-free current (of strength $λ$) and rotational current (of strength $ξ$). We find that the presence of rotational current $(ξ\neq 0)$ significantly affects growth kinetics. Depending on the parameter values, AMB+ exhibits either {\it macroscale phase separation} (MPS) or {\it microscale phase separation} ($μ$PS). We present detailed results for the kinetics of MPS and $μ$PS in AMB+ with critical composition.
