Miniaturized Low-Cost 3-D Printed High-Permittivity Dielectric Transmissive LP-to-CP Converter for L-Band Satellite Communications

Gazali Bashir, Javid Ahmad Ganie, Yazan Al-Alem, Hussein Attia

arXiv:2611.20624·eess.SP·Published 2026-11-16

A low-cost, miniaturized, additively manufactured transmissive linear-to-circular polarization converter for L-band air and space communication systems is presented. The proposed polarizer employs an in-house-fabricated high-permittivity dielectric medium, realized using a 3-D printed polylactic acid (PLA) enclosure filled with a TiO$_2$--water mixture. Variation in the effective permittivity is achieved by adjusting the mixture filling fraction. The selected 60\% filling fraction corresponds to an effective relative permittivity of 11.3 at 1 GHz. The high-permittivity loading enables miniaturization of the polarizer height and area by reducing the anisotropic dielectric unit-cell dimensions to $\boldsymbol{0.037λ_0 \times 0.037λ_0 \times 0.22λ_0}$. An additively manufactured polarizer with overall dimensions $\boldsymbol{0.83λ_0 \times 0.77λ_0 \times 0.22λ_0}$ is experimentally validated using a compact linearly polarized feed source. The measured results demonstrate LP-to-CP conversion, with a 3 dB axial ratio of 12\% and insertion loss of less than 0.6 dB. Radiation-pattern measurements at 1 GHz show LHCP-to-RHCP discrimination levels of 19 and 24 dB in the \textbf{\textit{xz}}- and \textbf{\textit{yz}}-planes, respectively. The proposed configuration combines miniaturization, LP-to-CP conversion, and low-cost additive manufacturing, making it promising for UAV-mounted and other size-constrained L-band satellite communication terminals.
