Rheology of Parametric Odd Materials

Pedro Cosme, Maziyar Jalaal

arXiv:2611.13969·cond-mat.soft·Published 2026-11-10

The design and control of rheological properties in active systems are crucial for developing functional materials with novel properties. While significant attention has been devoted to odd viscosity and odd elasticity, comprehensive odd rheological models remain scarce. Here, we introduce and characterise a platform-agnostic odd viscoelastic material, where complex responses arise from parametric control of internal rotational degrees of freedom and are triggered by coupling between normal and shear stresses. Such control can be achieved through either parametric driving or a parametric nonlinear feedback. We further show that this coupling can induce rotation-driven fluidisation, transitioning the material from an elasticity-dominated to a viscosity-dominated regime. Lastly, we show that the interplay between odd and even sectors enhances the nonlinear material response under large-amplitude oscillatory shear (LAOS). The findings outline a route to Floquet-engineered metafluids with programmable, non-reciprocal rheology.
