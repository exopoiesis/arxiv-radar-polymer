The critical exponent for semilinear wave equations with damped oscillations

Marcello D'Abbicco

arXiv:2608.11722·math.AP·Published 2026-08-12

We determine the existence exponent for the global small data solutions for a class of evolution equations with power nonlinearity under the effect of a dissipation that dampen the oscillations at low frequencies and produce overdamping at high frequencies. In the case of the wave equation with viscoelastic dissipation, the counterpart of blow-up in space dimension $n=3$ has been very recently proved by Wenhui Chen, showing that the existence exponent is optimal.
