Stability and Error Estimates for Convolution Quadrature under Radial Symbol Bounds

Jingchuang Ren, Haibin Su

arXiv:2610.23163·math.NA·Published 2026-10-16

We develop stability and error estimates for multistep convolution quadrature (CQ) under general radial bounds on the Laplace symbol. Instead of the classical single-power condition $|F(z)|\leq M|z|^{-μ}$, we assume $|F(z)|\leq CΦ(|z|)$ and consider two classes of radial functions: monotone bounds satisfying a scaling condition and regularly varying bounds with locally bounded above and away from zero. For strongly $A(α)$-stable multistep methods, we derive approximation and stability estimates for the CQ weights, as well as error bounds for power-type inputs $g_β(t)=t^{β-1}/Γ(β)$ with $β>0$. These estimates explicitly retain the dependence on the memory law through the factor $Φ(t_n^{-1})$. The framework accommodates symbols whose behavior cannot be described by a global single-power. It also preserves slowly varying and multiscale factors that would be lost under a coarser power majorant. The theory is applied to fractional viscoelastic models, distributed-order operators, and logarithmic-power memory operators. Numerical experiments with backward Euler and second-order backward differentiation CQ confirm the expected temporal convergence orders for direct responses. Complementary evolution examples demonstrate the corresponding temporal behavior for smooth and nonsmooth initial data.
