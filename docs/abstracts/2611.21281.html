A database of optical and thermal-radiative behaviour of materials under solar radiation

Vasiliki E. Alexopoulou, Nikolaos Karalis, Konstantinos Asimakopoulos, Andrei Totu, Constantin Sandu, Paul-Peter Sotiriadis

arXiv:2611.21281·cond-mat.mtrl-sci·Published 2026-11-16

Solar-matter interactions are important in many scientific and engineering applications. Modeling them needs consistent optical and thermal-radiative properties over the full solar spectrum. Here we present SpaderOptics, a database for 21 materials: metals, oxides, semiconductors, polymers, ceramics, and carbon. It compiles wavelength-dependent refractive indices and extinction coefficients from the literature. Each literature source is kept separate, together with its original conditions. One physics-based calculation chain turns these data into reflectivity, transmissivity, absorptivity, and emissivity for different incidence angles, slab thicknesses, and temperatures. Combined with the Air Mass Zero (AM0) solar spectrum, the properties give absorbed, reflected, and transmitted solar flux for concentrated sunlight. Equilibrium temperatures are given for both a theoretical lumped body and for a slab body, in which thermal conduction is considered. All data are limited to the solid state, up to a material-specific upper temperature limit. Currently, the database applies to extraterrestrial (AM0) sunlight only. A graphical App is also provided for exploring and visualizing the database.
