Modeling and analysis of a phase-field model for organic solar cells

Jan-Frederik Pietschmann, Carmen Tretmans, Nicola Zamponi

arXiv:2611.30660·math.AP·Published 2026-11-23

We derive and analyze a phase-field model describing the formation of the active layer of organic solar cells. The process combines phase separation of an electron donor (polymer) and an electron acceptor (non-fullerene acceptor) with evaporation of a solvent. Taking the macroscopic motion of the fluid into account, this results in a coupled system of incompressible Navier--Stokes, degenerate multispecies Cahn--Hilliard, and Allen--Cahn equations with cross-diffusion terms. We give a thermodynamically consistent derivation of the system and prove the existence of global-in-time weak solutions without any size constraints on the data. The solutions dissipate the total energy and require an ad-hoc variational formulation of the definition of the diffusion fluxes, which is made necessary by the degeneracy of the mobility matrix.
